Cancellation and Refund Policy

How cancellations, rescheduling and refunds work for sessions, workshops, cohorts and shop orders.

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Payments

By using the services offered by Kaha Mind, the user agrees to pay all applicable charges upfront. All payments are to be made to Kahamind Healthcare Private Limited.

Payment is supported in all online formats, including bank transfers, debit and credit cards (including HDFC Diners Club), PayPal and UPI.

Therapy sessions: cancellation and rescheduling

Kaha Mind allows rescheduling of an appointment, free of charge, up to 48 hours before the scheduled start of a session. Clients who wish to reschedule must inform the team at least 48 hours before the appointment. A refund will be initiated once due notice is given.

A request to reschedule a session that arrives less than 48 hours before the session will be considered a cancellation, and no refund will be made.

If we cancel

If Kaha Mind has to cancel or reschedule a session, you will not be charged for it, and we will offer you another time or a full refund.

Workshops and The Edge cohorts

If you withdraw before the start date, we will refund the fee paid, less any non-recoverable payment-gateway charges. Once a workshop or cohort has started, fees are generally non-refundable, but please write to us if you have an exceptional circumstance and we will consider it.

If we cancel or postpone a workshop or cohort, you can transfer your place to the next one or receive a full refund.

Shop products

Please check your order details before paying. If your item arrives damaged, defective or is not what you ordered, write to us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photo, and we will replace it or refund you. Items that have been worn, washed or used cannot be returned. You can cancel an order before it has been dispatched for a full refund.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method. We process them within 5 business days of approval, and your bank or card issuer may take a further 5 to 10 business days to show the credit.

How to request a cancellation or refund

Email us at hello@kahamind.com or message us on WhatsApp at +91 92668 70877. Please include your name, the email or phone number you booked with, what you booked, and the date of the session or your order number.
