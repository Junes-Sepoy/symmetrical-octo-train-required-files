Cancellation and Refund Policy

How rescheduling, cancellation and refunds work for therapy sessions at Kaha Mind.

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Scheduling a session

Send us a message on WhatsApp at +91 92668 70877. Once we match you with the right therapist, we will help set up your first session at a time that works for you.

You will receive an email confirmation from Kaha Mind Therapist (therapy@kahamind.com) with your meeting link. On the day of your session, simply click the link to join. If you do not see the email, please check your spam folder, and if it is still missing, let us know before your session and we will sort it out right away.

Rescheduling a session

Send us a message on +91 92668 70877 and we will help reschedule your session. After rescheduling, you will receive a new email from Kaha Mind Therapist (therapy@kahamind.com) with an updated meeting link. Please use this new link to join.

Because therapists plan their weeks in advance, sessions can only be rescheduled up to 24 hours beforehand.

Rescheduling within 24 hours

Rescheduling within 24 hours of the session is treated as a cancellation with a 100% fee, and you will need to book a new slot.

Cancelling a session

To cancel a session, reach us on +91 92668 70877 or at hello@kahamind.com, and our team will guide you through the cancellation process.

Refunds

Cancellations made earlier than 24 hours before the session are fully refundable. Cancellations made within 24 hours of the session carry a 100% fee and are not refundable.

Contact us

For any question about rescheduling, cancellation or refunds, write to us at hello@kahamind.com or message us on WhatsApp at +91 92668 70877.
