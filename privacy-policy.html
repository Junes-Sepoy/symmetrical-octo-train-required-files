Privacy Policy

What personal information we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Who we are

Kahamind Healthcare Private Limited, trading as Kaha Mind (“Kaha Mind”, “we”, “us”).
CIN: U85300KA2018PTC111157
Registered office: No. 516, Krishvi Aspire, Binnamangala Extension, CMH Road, Indiranagar 1st Stage, Bangalore, Karnataka 560038

This policy explains what personal information we collect through kahamind.com and our booking system, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We are the data fiduciary for the information described here.

What we collect

Depending on how you use our website and services, we may collect:

  • Contact details you give us, such as your name, email address, phone number and organisation, when you fill in a contact or enquiry form, subscribe to our newsletter, or book a session or workshop.
  • Booking and session administration details, such as the service you booked, appointment times and payment status.
  • Delivery details for shop orders: name, address, PIN code, phone number and email.
  • Information you choose to include in a message to us, including anything about why you are seeking support.
  • Basic technical information such as browser type and pages visited, to keep the website working.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your enquiries, arrange and deliver sessions, workshops and cohorts, process and deliver orders, send receipts and confirmations, send the newsletter if you have asked for it, keep records we are required to keep, and improve the website and our services. We do not sell your personal information.

Therapy records

Information shared in therapy is treated as confidential and is handled under the professional and ethical standards that apply to mental health practitioners. It is kept separately from website and marketing information, and is not used for marketing.

Payments

Online payments are handled by Razorpay and other payment partners. Your card, UPI and bank details are entered on their secure pages and are never seen or stored by Kaha Mind. We receive only confirmation that a payment was made, and its reference.

Who we share it with

We share information only with people and services that help us run Kaha Mind, and only as far as they need it: our practitioners, our booking and customer-management tools (including Zoho), our form and email providers, our payment partners, our website hosting provider and delivery partners for shop orders. We may also disclose information where the law requires it, or where it is necessary to protect someone from serious harm.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected, and for as long as the law or our professional obligations require. After that, we delete it or anonymise it.

Keeping it safe

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including secure connections and limiting access to the people who need it. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, and you can withdraw your consent at any time, including unsubscribing from the newsletter using the link in any issue. To do any of this, write to us at hello@kahamind.com. Some information we may need to keep where the law or our professional duties require it.

Withdrawing consent

Deleting certain information or withdrawing your consent may lead to cancellation of your access to the website or to certain features and services.

Cookies

Our website uses only the cookies and similar technologies it needs to work, and those used by our embedded services, such as payments and fonts. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Children

Our website is not directed at children. Where a parent or guardian books a session for someone under 18, we collect only what is needed for that service.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.

Contact us

Write to us at hello@kahamind.com or message us on WhatsApp at +91 92668 70877.
