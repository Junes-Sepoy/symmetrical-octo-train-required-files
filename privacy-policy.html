Privacy Policy

How Kaha Mind collects, uses, shares and protects your information.

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Scope

This website, under the name and style “Kaha Mind”, is developed and made available by Kahamind Healthcare Private Limited, with its registered office at No. 516, Krishvi Aspire, Binnamangala Extension, CMH Road, Indiranagar 1st Stage, Bangalore, Karnataka 560038, India. “We”, “us” and “our” include our affiliates, associates, assignees and successors-in-interest.

This Privacy Policy, read with our Terms and Conditions, explains how we collect, use and safeguard your information through the website and our services. It is an electronic record under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under it, and does not require any physical or digital signature.

Children

Anyone under 18 is treated as a child and needs parental consent to use the website. “You” means the parent or legal guardian of a child, or a user over 18. Clicking “I agree” and submitting personal information, whether by you, or by a child with a parent’s or guardian’s permission, means you accept this Privacy Policy.

Where the user is a minor, the parent or guardian is the primary account holder. They are responsible for ordering services and paying fees, for deciding whether to share the child’s content with us and third parties, and for monitoring that content. Children must not include personally identifiable information in anything they post unless a parent or guardian has given permission, and we assume any such information was shared with that permission.

Your consent

We need your consent before giving you access to the website and its services. Under applicable law we cannot collect, process or store information about you without it, and without it we cannot provide our services.

Information we collect

Personal information is information that can identify you or your child. It may include:

  • contact and background details, such as name, age, gender, contact information and educational background;
  • transaction details for services you pay for. We do not collect your card number, cardholder name, expiry date, CVV or internet banking credentials;
  • session log information, such as how you navigate the website, source of traffic, IP address (from which we can roughly work out location), browser type and language, operating system, and date and time of request;
  • your communications with us on the website.

Most of this is provided by you when you register or use our services. Some is collected automatically as you browse, through cookies, web beacons and similar technologies, and we may also receive information about you from third parties and publicly available sources. We may collect personal information for behavioural advertising, and may allow third-party behavioural trackers in areas intended for children.

Sensitive personal information, such as passwords and payment instrument details, may be collected only to enable payment for services.

How we use it

We use your information to process your requests for our services, help you choose the services that suit you, improve the website and our services, run our internal operations and meet legal and regulatory requirements, carry out quality audits and training of our trainers, conduct market analysis and research, handle enquiries and complaints, provide customer care, and develop new features.

Sharing and disclosure

We may share your information with our affiliates where needed to perform services on our behalf or yours. We or our affiliates may market to you as a result, unless you opt out by emailing the Grievance Officer from your registered email address.

We may share personal or sensitive personal information with outside organisations or individuals where reasonably necessary to detect, prevent or address fraud, security or technical issues, or to protect the rights, property or safety of our customers or the public, as required or permitted by law.

We may also disclose your information in response to court orders, legal process or tax authorities, which may be without notice to you, and to third parties to whom we transfer our rights and duties under an agreement.

We do not sell personal data about you or your child for marketing, advertising or other third-party use, except with your express consent.

Retention

We keep information only for as long as needed for the purposes described here, unless the law requires or permits longer. Once the purpose is achieved, personal information is deleted in a safe and secure way. We have adopted reasonable security practices and procedures as required by law, including strategic, operational, managerial, technical, physical and encryption controls.

Non-personal information

Non-personal information does not identify you or your child, and may include system or hardware identifiers and software information. When combined with personal information, it is treated as personal information.

We may collect diagnostic, technical and usage information, anonymised so that it does not identify you, to provide and improve the website, supply software updates and check compliance with this policy. We may share a subset of it, in a form that does not identify you, with partners and third-party developers so they can improve software, hardware and services designed for use with the website. We keep it only as long as needed and then delete it securely.

Cookies

Information collected by cookies and similar technologies is treated as non-personal information. Where IP addresses or similar identifiers count as personal information under local law, we treat information linked to them as personal information. We do not use cookies to save personal information for unauthorised purposes.

Access, correction and deletion

You are responsible for making sure the information you give us is correct, current and updated, and that you have the right to provide it. You can ask to review, update, amend or delete your personal information by emailing hello@kahamind.com.

Deleting information or withdrawing consent may end your access to the website or to certain features and services. We may not be able to act on a request where it is not supported by valid documents, where the law, law enforcement or judicial proceedings require us to keep the data, where it is extremely difficult to implement (for example, backup copies), or where it would risk the privacy of other users.

Security

We have appropriate methods and managerial procedures to protect your personal information, and we process it only in ways compatible with the purpose it was collected for. We take commercially reasonable precautions against loss, theft, unauthorised access, disclosure, reproduction, use or amendment, including sharing access only to the minimum extent necessary, using secure servers, and verifying identity before granting access to personal information.

Sending data over the internet carries inherent security risks, and you use the website at your own risk. We are not liable for disclosures caused by transmission errors, unauthorised third-party access or other events beyond our reasonable control. If we become aware of a breach of the security of your information, we will promptly notify you and take appropriate action to remedy it.

Data transfers

Your information may be stored and processed in India or in any other country where we, our subsidiaries, affiliates or service providers maintain facilities, and may be transferred to affiliated entities or other third parties across borders. Those countries may have data protection laws that differ from yours and may not be as protective. By submitting information on the website you agree to this, and we take steps, including signed agreements, to keep your information protected in line with this policy.

Third-party services

Your internet service provider, operating system provider, third-party applications, social media platforms and websites you visit may also collect, use and share information about you. We cannot control how they do this, so please read their privacy policies. We are not liable for third-party products, software or services, and you assume the risks of any transaction you make with a third party through the website.

Exclusions

This policy covers only information collected by the website. It does not apply to unsolicited information you send us through the website or by any other means, including anything posted in public areas of the website. Unsolicited information is treated as non-confidential, and we are free to use and disclose it.

Access to your personal information is limited to employees, agents, partners and third parties who need it to provide services to you. We are not responsible for how our partners and third parties handle your information outside the scope of our agreements with them.

Liability

We are not liable for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special or exemplary damages arising from loss of data or use in connection with your access to, or inability to access, the website, including its sub-domains.

Sale, merger or acquisition

We may share your information with our parent, subsidiaries and affiliates for internal purposes. We may also disclose and transfer it to a later owner or operator of the website or its database, or in connection with a merger, consolidation, restructuring, sale of substantially all of our interests or assets, or other corporate change, including during due diligence. We will notify you by email and/or a prominent notice on the website of any change in ownership.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our business or in legal and regulatory requirements, and will publish the update online. We will make efforts to tell you about significant changes, but please review this page from time to time. Continuing to use the website or services after an update means you accept it.

Queries and complaints

If you have questions about this policy, or about how we collect, use and disclose information, you can contact our Grievance Officer at hello@kahamind.com. The Grievance Officer will address your grievances expeditiously.
